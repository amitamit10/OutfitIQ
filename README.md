# OutfitIQ

OutfitIQ is a smart wardrobe app that helps people decide what to wear without spending too much time looking through their closet.

The idea came from a simple problem: many of us own plenty of clothes but still struggle to choose an outfit. OutfitIQ helps organize your clothes and suggests outfits based on what you already have.

## What it can do

- Add and organize clothing items
- Group clothes by category, color, season, and style
- Get outfit suggestions
- Plan outfits for different occasions
- Create packing lists for trips
- Track clothes that are being washed
- See which clothes are used most often

## How AI is used

OutfitIQ uses AI to help identify clothing items and suggest outfit combinations. The recommendations can take the user's wardrobe, preferences, weather, and occasion into account.

AI suggestions are meant to be helpful, but users can always change the details or choose a different outfit.

## Why I built it

I wanted to build something practical that could make a small everyday decision easier. Instead of buying more clothes or forgetting what is already in the closet, OutfitIQ helps users make better use of what they already own.
