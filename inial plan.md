# 👔 OutfitIQ – AI Smart Wardrobe

## Overview

OutfitIQ is an AI-powered digital wardrobe that helps users organize their clothes, build outfits, plan packing for trips, and decide what to wear every day.

Instead of standing in front of a closet wondering what to wear, users simply open OutfitIQ and receive personalized outfit recommendations based on their wardrobe, weather, schedule, and preferences.

The app also helps users manage laundry, avoid buying duplicate clothes, track wardrobe usage, and build packing lists for vacations.

---

# The Problem

Most people:

* Own a lot of clothes
* Wear only a small percentage of them
* Forget what they already own
* Buy duplicate items
* Spend time every morning choosing clothes
* Pack inefficiently for trips
* Keep clothes they never wear

---

# The Solution

OutfitIQ creates a digital version of the user's wardrobe using AI.

The app helps users:

* Scan clothes
* Organize everything automatically
* Build outfits
* Plan travel packing
* Track clothing usage
* Receive fashion recommendations
* Discover forgotten clothes

# AI Closet Scanner

## Overview

Users take a photo of a clothing item.

AI automatically detects:

* Clothing type
* Color
* Pattern
* Season
* Style
* Formality
* Material (if possible)
* Brand (if visible)

Example:

```
White T-Shirt

Category:
Shirt

Season:
Summer

Style:
Casual

Color:
White
```

If AI is unsure, users can edit the information before saving.

# Product Vision

OutfitIQ is more than a wardrobe app.

It becomes a personal fashion assistant that knows everything the user owns and helps them make better clothing decisions every day.

Instead of asking:

> "What should I wear today?"

Users simply open OutfitIQ and receive a complete, personalized recommendation in seconds.

The long-term vision is to become the central hub for wardrobe management, travel packing, clothing organization, and AI-powered personal styling.
